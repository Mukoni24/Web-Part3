News & Events

Volunteer Appreciation Event

Date: 12 March 2026

Angela’s Hope Foundation hosted a special event to express gratitude and appreciation for the dedicated volunteers. who contribute their time and efforts to support the foundation's initiatives. Residents were encouraged to join the foundation as volunteers and make a positive impact in their community.

Donation Drive

Date: 5 May 2026

Angela’s Hope Foundation organized a donation drive to collect essential items for families in need. The drive focused on gathering clothing, non-perishable food items, and school supplies.

Community Clean-up Campaign

Date: 10 June 2026

Residents and volunteers combined their efforts to clean up common areas. This campaign encouraged people to be more responsible about their surroundings and created a cleaner environment for families in the area.

Community Food Drive

Date: 15 July 2026

Our volunteers united to ensure that food packages were distributed to people who lacked food security. The project ensured that people were offered necessary foods while encouraging interaction among the community members.
